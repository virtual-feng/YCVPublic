# Privacy Policy for YCVAiComposer

**Effective Date:** [November 25, 2026]

## 1. Overview
YCVAiComposer ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. All video processing, computer vision analysis, and machine learning inference occur **100% offline and locally on your iOS device**. We do not operate external servers to process your videos, nor do we collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
Because the App operates entirely on your device:
- **No Video or Media Uploads:** Your basketball footage, imported video clips, and exported highlight videos never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export them using iOS system sharing features.
- **No Personal Accounts or Tracking:** The App does not require user registration and does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies.
- **No Cloud Telemetry:** Machine learning model inference runs exclusively on your device's hardware (CPU, GPU, and Apple Neural Engine) without sending frames, metadata, or usage logs to external servers.

## 3. Device Permissions
To perform its core video composition functions, the App requests access to the following iOS permissions:
- **Photo Library Access:** Used solely to let you select source videos from your Photos library and save your composed highlight videos back to your device.
- **Local File Access:** Used solely when you import video files from the iOS Files app or AirDrop for local editing.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your iPhone's **Settings > Privacy & Security**.

## 4. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. While the App may be used to compose footage of junior sports matches, all video data remains strictly under the user's control on their local device.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
